Design a responsive e-commerce home page for "Xana Life", a Kenyan shop in Nairobi with five divisions: Pharmacy, Retail, Deli, Liquor and Wholesale. Produce two screens: Desktop (1440 px wide) and Mobile (390 px wide). Follow every value below exactly. Do not invent sections, copy, colours, icons, illustrations, gradients, shadows or animations that are not described here. If something is not specified, leave it out rather than adding it.

OVERALL STYLE
- Calm, plain, practical grocery-shop look, like a real supermarket website. It must not look AI-generated: no gradients, no glows, no decorative shapes, no blobs, no swooshes, no hero illustration, no icons in front of headings, no small label text above headings, no hover lift or zoom effects, no emoji.
- One accent colour: green. Yellow is used only for discount badges and one small dot in the mobile offer bar.
- Page background is a very light grey-green. All content sits in white boxes with thin borders. No drop shadows on cards.

COLOURS (use these hex values exactly)

- Dark green (button hover): #035A30
- Gold: #F79F26 (not used on this screen except in the logo)

- Active wholesale tier row: background #EAF3EC, text #035A30

TYPOGRAPHY
- Headings, prices and buttons: "Libre Franklin", bold (700) or extra bold (800), letter spacing -0.02em.
- Body text: "Atkinson Hyperlegible Next" (if unavailable, use "Atkinson Hyperlegible"), regular 400, base size 15 px, line height 1.55.
- All digits, including prices, use Libre Franklin, so zeros are not slashed.

SHAPES
- Cards and large boxes: 14 px corner radius.
- Buttons, inputs, chips and small controls: 10 px corner radius.
- Badges: 6 px radius. The "Sponsored" label and the count pill are fully rounded.
- Borders are 1 px solid #DCE4DB.

ICONS
